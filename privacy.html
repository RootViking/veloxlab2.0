Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026.

This page describes what happens to information you share with VeloxLab Studio through this website and, if you become a client, through the Instagram booking agent.

Contact form

When you send the contact form or the free spec-ad form, the message is delivered to us by FormSubmit and arrives in our email. We use it to reply to you and to make the spec ad you asked for. We don't sell it.

Booking a call

The 15-minute call is booked through Calendly. Calendly collects the details you enter on their booking page and handles that appointment.

Instagram booking agent

If you use our Instagram booking agent, your customers' messages stay inside Instagram and the messaging platform that runs the agent, in an account in your business's name. We use them only to run and improve your agent and to count results for your monthly report. We don't sell or share them, and we don't copy them into other tools. When our work ends, we remove our access.

How to reach us

Questions about this page can be sent to sadielalmanza@outlook.com.
