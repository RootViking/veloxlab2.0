Questions

Who owns the accounts?

You do. The agent runs on an account in your name, billed to your card, so it stays yours.

What happens with medical or clinical questions?

The agent doesn't answer them. It hands anything clinical to you.

What do I need to do?

One onboarding call, your prices and FAQs, and access to your Instagram. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

Is everything in Spanish too?

Every ad and every answer is written natively in English and Spanish, not translated.

Do I need a website?

No. If you don't have one, a one-page bilingual booking page is included as a bonus.

Do you promise a number of bookings?

No. We don't promise bookings or revenue. Your monthly report shows DMs answered, booking links sent and the bookings you confirm.

Is there a contract?

The plan runs for 3 months, then continues month to month.

How much does it cost?

We go over pricing on the 15-minute call, once we know your business.

How soon is the agent live?

Within 7 days of getting your info and Instagram access. If not, your first month is free.

Who owns the account? Do you need my Instagram password?

The account is yours, set up in your business's name. You connect it to your Instagram yourself through Meta's official login, so we never ask for your password. You add us as a team member and can remove us anytime.

Can I turn the agent off?

Yes, anytime. You can pause it in one conversation and answer that person yourself, or switch it off for your whole Instagram. If you leave after your plan ends, you keep the account and setup in your name or we turn it off, and either way we remove our access.

What happens to my clients' messages?

They stay inside Instagram and the messaging platform the agent runs on. We use them only to run and improve your agent and to count results for your monthly report. We don't sell or share them, and we don't copy them into other tools.

Is there any other cost?

The messaging platform has its own small monthly fee, billed to your business. We tell you the exact amount on the call.
